Financial institutions nationwide are facing a growing challenge of remaining compliant while evolving in an ever-increasing, cost-pressure environment. According to a recent survey from Accenture , the overwhelming majority of compliance executives expect the cost of compliance to rise as much as 30% over the next two years, yet only six out of ten expect an increase in operational funding. Meanwhile, the cost of non-compliance has never been higher.
